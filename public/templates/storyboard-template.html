Module Duration: 20-25 minutes
Date: March 26, 2025
Version: 1.0

Module Overview

This module teaches employees how to identify phishing attempts and respond appropriately to protect organizational data. Through interactive examples, realistic simulations, and practical guidelines, learners will develop the skills to recognize and properly handle suspicious emails and messages.

Learning Objectives:

  • Identify at least 5 common indicators of phishing attempts with 100% accuracy
  • Determine the appropriate response to different types of suspicious communications
  • Execute the organization's reporting procedure for suspected phishing attempts
  • Apply precautionary measures to prevent data compromise
